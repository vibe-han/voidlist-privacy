Void List Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Void List is a to-do app that runs entirely on your phone. It does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What is stored

Your Todos, Series, Badges and settings are stored only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers, and nothing you enter ever leaves your phone.

What is not collected

Void List has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. It makes no network requests.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set for your Todos.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so those reminders arrive on time.

Both are used only for reminders you schedule yourself, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Void List deletes all of its data. The app is excluded from phone backups, so your data can't be restored afterwards. You can also reset Todos or Badges from the app's Settings.

Purchases

Void List is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer never sees your payment details.

Children

Because Void List collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
