Void List Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Void List app for Android. I'm Vihan A, the developer who publishes Void List on Google Play, and I'm responsible for how the app handles personal data.

In short, everything you put into Void List stays on your phone. The app doesn't collect your data, send it to me, or share it with anyone.

1. What the app stores on your phone

Void List keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the Todos and Series you create, with their names, due times, and completion and postpone history
  • your Badge progress
  • your settings, such as theme and reminder preferences

There are no user accounts and I run no servers. Nothing in the app sends this information over the internet, and I have no way to see it.

2. What the app doesn't collect

Void List has no analytics, ads, tracking or crash reporting, and it doesn't include third-party software that collects data. It doesn't collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data.

3. Permissions

Void List asks for two permissions. Notifications let it show the reminders you set for your Todos. Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) let those reminders arrive at the time you picked.

The app uses them only for reminders you schedule, and neither one gives me access to anything. You can turn both off in your phone's settings, and the app still works without them.

4. What Google Play handles

Void List is sold and distributed through Google Play. Google processes your purchase and payment, and may collect information about your installation, under the Google Privacy Policy. I never receive your card or payment details.

Google shares some of what it collects with me through the Play Console:

  • order records for purchases of the app, such as order number, date, price and country, which I use only for refunds, tax and accounting
  • aggregated, anonymised statistics, including install counts, ratings, and crash and performance reports from people who chose to share diagnostics with Google

I don't use any of this to identify, contact or track you.

5. If you email me

If you email me for support, I get your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report. The email stays in my Proton Mail inbox, which Proton AG hosts in Switzerland, for as long as I need it to handle your request. I delete it within 12 months after our conversation ends, or sooner if you ask. I don't share it with anyone. Emailing me is optional.

6. Selling and sharing

I don't sell, rent or share personal information with anyone, and I don't use it for advertising.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Data in the app stays on your device until you delete it. You can reset your Todos or Badges from the app's Settings at any time, and uninstalling Void List permanently deletes all of its data.

Void List opts out of Android backup, so its data isn't copied to your Google account and can't be restored after you uninstall.

Support emails are kept as described in section 5.

8. Security

Android keeps Void List's storage separate from other apps, and the app never sends that data over a network. Beyond that, your data is as safe as your phone is, so your screen lock and device encryption matter too.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. The app's data never leaves your device, so you control it directly. For any support emails I hold, write to me at the address below and I'll respond within 30 days.

10. Children

Void List isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal data from anyone, children included. If a child has emailed me, a parent or guardian can ask me to delete that email.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version on this page with a new "last updated" date. If a change affects how your data is handled, for example if the app ever starts collecting data, I'll describe it clearly here before it takes effect.
