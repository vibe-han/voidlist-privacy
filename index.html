Void List Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated 11 November 2026

This policy explains how the Void List Android app ("Void List", "the app") handles information. Void List is developed and published on Google Play by Vihan A ("the developer", "I", "me"), who is responsible for the app's handling of personal data.

In short: everything you enter into Void List stays on your phone. The app does not collect your data, send it to the developer, or share it with anyone.

1. Information stored on your device

Void List stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Todos and Series you create, including their names, due times, and completion and postpone history
  • Badge progress
  • Your settings, such as theme and reminder preferences

There are no user accounts and no developer servers. The app has no feature that sends this information over the internet, and the developer has no access to it.

2. Information the app does not collect

Void List contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party software that collects data. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data.

3. Device permissions

  • Notifications: used to show the reminders you set for your Todos.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): used so those reminders arrive at the time you chose.

These permissions are used only for reminders you schedule. They do not give the developer access to any information. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings, and the app keeps working without them.

4. Information handled by Google Play

Void List is distributed and sold through Google Play. Google processes your purchase and payment, and may collect information about your installation, under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer never receives your card or payment details.

Through the Google Play Console, Google gives the developer limited information it collects, specifically:

  • order records for purchases of the app (such as order number, date, price and country), used only for refunds, tax and accounting, and
  • aggregated, anonymised statistics such as install counts, ratings, and crash and performance reports from users who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google.

The developer does not use this information to identify, contact or track you.

5. If you contact me

If you email me for support, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem you report. Emails are kept in my Proton Mail inbox for as long as needed to handle your request and are deleted within 12 months after the conversation ends, or sooner if you ask. Proton AG (Switzerland) hosts the mailbox. I do not share your email with anyone else. Contacting me is optional.

6. Sharing and sale of information

I do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with third parties, and I do not use it for advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Data in the app stays on your device until you delete it. You can reset Todos or Badges from the app's Settings at any time. Uninstalling Void List permanently deletes all of its data.
  • Backups: Void List opts out of Android backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account and cannot be restored after you uninstall.
  • Support emails are kept as described in section 5.

8. Security

Void List's data is kept in app-private storage that Android isolates from other apps. It is never sent over a network. Its protection also depends on your device's own security, such as your screen lock and device encryption.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the app's data never leaves your device, you control it directly. For any support emails I hold, contact me at the address below and I will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

Void List is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If a child has emailed me, a parent or guardian can ask me to delete that email.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. If a change affects how your data is handled, for example if the app ever begins to collect data, the change will be clearly described here before it takes effect.
